The **Datathon** is a hackathon organized by STADS, where teams of students develop data-driven solutions for real-world business challenges within a limited timeframe.

Companies provide real datasets and challenges — participants analyze, model, and present their results to an expert jury. Creativity, analytical thinking, and teamwork take center stage.

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced data scientist — the Datathon offers a unique opportunity to gain hands-on experience, connect with like-minded peers, and win exciting prizes.
