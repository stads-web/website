As a student initiative at the University of Mannheim, STADS unites **motivated students** with **strong analytical and quantitative backgrounds** and a shared **passion for Data Science**.

We connect academia and industry by working on real-world projects, offering students hands-on experience while delivering valuable insights to our partners.

Through **workshops, events, and close collaboration**, we aim to bridge the gap between theory and practice — fostering the next generation of data-driven talent.
