We're a student initiative at the University of Mannheim that brings together curious minds with a **passion for Data Science** and quantitative thinking.

At STADS, we learn from each other, work on real projects with our partners from academia and industry, and grow as a team at workshops, events, and our **legendary Monday Sessions**.

Whether you're deep into data or just starting out — you'll find people here who love to learn, build cool things, and have a great time doing it.
